**Description (472/500):**

Generator Tracker is a logbook for power generators. Tap Start/Stop and engine hours count themselves; enter the tank size to see fuel and runtime left, with litres per hour from your refills; set oil, filter and spark plug intervals by hours or days and see what's overdue on the home screen; monthly charts per generator. Offline-first, account optional, open source (MIT). Made in Ukraine for scheduled blackouts — useful for any backup, off-grid or job-site generator.

**First comment** (true once iOS 3.1 is approved and free):

Hi Product Hunt 👋

I'm Yuri, a developer from Ukraine. With scheduled blackouts, a generator here is an everyday appliance — and the questions are always the same: how many hours has it run, when is the next oil change, how much fuel is left and how much does it burn per hour? Most people keep a paper notebook or nothing at all.

Generator Tracker answers those with as few taps as possible:
⚡ Start/Stop from the home screen — engine hours count themselves, several generators can run at once, Undo if you stopped by mistake
⛽ Fuel left and runtime estimate from the tank size; litres per hour from your refills
🔧 Maintenance by engine hours, days or both — overdue tasks show on the home screen
📊 Monthly charts per generator
🌙 Big buttons for the dark and a true black theme that saves battery during outages
🔒 Offline-first: everything stays on the phone; an account is optional and only syncs your own devices. Open source (MIT).

Editions: on iPhone/iPad it's free, with a small banner on two screens that a one-time purchase removes (no subscriptions). On Android there's the full paid app with no ads, and a free Lite version in closed testing — testers welcome: https://groups.google.com/g/generator-tracker-lite

Built with React Native/Expo and Firebase. I'd love your feedback: what would make it useful for your generator — at home, on a site or in a rental fleet?
