What's free

What you get in the free version

The free version does the whole job. The limits are stated here in full, so nothing surprises you after you install it.

Everything for tracking

One-tap Start and Stop, run hours, fuel left in the tank, refills, maintenance reminders by hours or days, and monthly charts.

Up to 3 generators

The free version holds up to 3 generators and 2 maintenance tasks for each one. Records you already have are never hidden or deleted.

Ads on two screens only

Ads appear on the Analytics and Settings screens. Never on the home screen, never around Start and Stop, never at launch.

Pay once, if you want

“Remove ads” $0.99, “Unlimited” $0.99, or “Everything” $1.49. One-time purchases, no subscription, restored on your other devices.

FAQ

Questions about downloading

Is Generator Tracker free?

Yes. Generator Tracker for iPhone and iPad and Generator Tracker Lite for Android are free to download and use. They show ads on two screens and hold up to 3 generators with 2 maintenance tasks each. You can remove the ads or the limits with a one-time purchase: $0.99 each, or $1.49 for both.

What is the difference between Generator Tracker Lite and Generator Tracker on Android?

They are the same app. Generator Tracker Lite is free, with ads and limits that you can remove once. Generator Tracker is the paid edition: you pay one time in Google Play and it has no ads, no limits and no in-app purchases.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. Everything is stored on your phone and works offline. An account is optional and only syncs your own records between your devices.

Is there a subscription?

No. There are no subscriptions. Every purchase is paid once and stays with your App Store or Google Play account.

Which phones and tablets does it work on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 16.4 or newer, and Android phones, tablets and foldables with Android 7.0 or newer.

Who makes the app, and can I trust it?

Generator Tracker is made by Yuri Valigursky, an independent developer from Ukraine, where power cuts made generators part of daily life. The app is open source under the MIT license, so anyone can read the code on GitHub. It has no analytics or tracking of its own; ads ask for consent where the law requires it.
