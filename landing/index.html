Features

Everything You Need to Track

Powerful tools designed for real-world generator management, from construction sites to home backup power.

Session Tracking

Start and stop sessions with one tap. Track real-time elapsed hours or log sessions manually with precise time entries.

Fuel Logging

Record every refill with date and amount. Calculate fuel consumption rates automatically based on operating hours.

Analytics Dashboard

View total hours, fuel consumption, and efficiency metrics. Compare performance across multiple generators.

Multi-Generator Support

Manage unlimited generators from a single dashboard. Perfect for fleets, rental businesses, and facilities.

Cloud Sync

Optional Firebase sync keeps your data safe across devices. Works offline-first—sync when you have connection.

Dark Mode

Automatic theme switching based on system preferences. Easy on the eyes during night-time checks.
