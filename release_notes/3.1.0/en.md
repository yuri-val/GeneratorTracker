# Release Notes 3.1.0

**Date:** 2026-10-17
**Version:** 3.1.0

## Overview
Generator Tracker now comes in two editions. The paid Android app stays exactly as it is: no ads, no limits. New are the free editions — the iOS app, which is now free, and **Generator Tracker Lite** on Android, coming after testing on Google Play — where ads and a few limits can be removed with one-time purchases.

## ✨ What's New
- **Free editions**: the iOS app is free to install and use; Generator Tracker Lite (Android) follows after testing on Google Play.
- **One-time purchases** in the free editions: "Remove ads" and "Unlimited" at $0.99 each, or "Everything" at $1.49. No subscriptions; purchases are tied to your store account and can be restored on any device (Settings → Purchases).
- **Ads, kept out of the way**: a banner on the Analytics and Settings screens and at most one full-screen ad when opening Analytics, no more than once every 10 minutes. Never on the home screen, around Start/Stop, on the generator screen or at launch.
- **Limits of the free editions**: up to 3 generators and 2 maintenance tasks per generator. Anything already on your device or synced from your account stays visible and usable; the limit only applies to adding more.
- **Privacy**: ads ask for your consent where the law requires it, and the app itself still sends nothing about you to anyone. The privacy policy has a new "Advertising" section.

## 🔧 Changed
- "Session Running" on the generator screen is now "Session running", like the other labels.

## 🐛 Fixed
- **Sign in with Google on Android**: the button is back in Settings → Account and opens the familiar Google account picker.
