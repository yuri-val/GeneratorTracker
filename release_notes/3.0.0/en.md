# Release Notes 3.0.0

**Date:** 2026-10-10
**Version:** 3.0.0

## Overview
Generator Tracker 3.0 is redesigned around what you do during a power cut: start the generator in one tap, keep an eye on it, and never miss maintenance.

## ✨ What's New
- **Start and Stop from the home screen**: running generators move to the top with a live timer, fuel left and a big Stop button. Several generators can run at once.
- **Undo instead of "Are you sure?"**: Stop works immediately; tap Undo within 5 seconds if it was a mistake.
- **Fuel left**: add your tank size in the generator settings to see roughly how much fuel and how many hours are left. Marking a refill "filled to full" keeps the estimate accurate.
- **New refill sheet**: a stepper, quick presets (to full, same as last time) and a reminder to turn the engine off first — "Stop and refill" ends the session for you. After saving, "Start again" is one tap away.
- **Generator screen**: a live clock, fuel used and left, engine hours that include the running session, and maintenance bars showing how much of each service interval is used.
- **Other generators at a glance**: a bar at the bottom shows what else is running, with a Stop button.
- **New look**: a warm light theme and a true-black dark theme that saves battery, clear high-contrast buttons, and a theme setting (light, dark or system). Settings also explain how to turn on a red screen at night.
- **New app icon.**

## 🔧 Changed
- Starting, stopping and deleting no longer show confirmation pop-ups.
