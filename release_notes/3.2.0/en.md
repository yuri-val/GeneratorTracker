# Release Notes 3.2.0

**Date:** 2026-10-25
**Version:** 3.2.0

## Overview
Generator Tracker now speaks gallons. Fuel can be shown in litres or US gallons, and the app picks the unit that fits your region on its own.

## ✨ What's New
- **Litres or US gallons**: choose in Settings → Fuel units. On devices set to the United States the app starts in gallons; everywhere else it starts in litres.
- **Everywhere at once**: tank size, refills, fuel left, consumption per hour and the monthly fuel chart all follow the chosen unit.
- **Refills in gallons**: quick buttons for ½ gal or 5 gal (depending on the tank size), and the ± stepper moves by 0.1 or 1 gal.
- **Nothing to convert by hand**: records you already have are simply shown in the new unit. Your data does not change, and switching back and forth is safe — even across devices that use different units.

Thanks to everyone on Reddit and the DIY Solar Forum who asked for this.
