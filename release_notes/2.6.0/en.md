# Release Notes 2.6.0

**Date:** 2026-10-06
**Version:** 2.6.0

## Overview
You can now delete your account right in the app. Your records stay on your phone.

## ✨ What's New
- **Delete your account in Settings**: removes your account and everything synced to the cloud — generators, sessions, refills and maintenance tasks. Email accounts confirm with their password.
- **Your data stays on the device**: after deletion, everything on your phone keeps working offline. If you sign in again later, it is uploaded to the new account.

## 🔧 Changed
- On iPhone, sign-in is available with email or anonymously.
