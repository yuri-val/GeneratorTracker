What the App Does

Generator Tracker is a logbook for power generators. It records run sessions and refills, counts engine hours, estimates fuel left, runtime and consumption per hour, and reminds you about maintenance by engine hours and/or days. It works offline; an account is only needed for cloud sync.

Estimates and Your Responsibility

The figures in the app are calculated from the data you enter (start and stop times, refills, tank size, service intervals). They can differ from reality: actual fuel consumption depends on load, temperature, the engine’s condition and other factors, and a reminder can only be as accurate as the times and intervals you set.

  • The app does not replace your generator’s manual, its hour meter or the manufacturer’s service schedule.
  • Check the oil and fuel level yourself and follow the manufacturer’s safety instructions (carbon monoxide, refuelling, electrical connection).
  • Decisions about operating, refuelling and servicing your equipment are your responsibility.
